01

Who we are

Nexyra Consulting Ltd ("Nexyra", "we", "us" or "our") is responsible for the personal information described in this policy. We are a consultancy based in Slough, Berkshire, United Kingdom.

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your information, please email hello@nexyraconsulting.co.uk.

02

Information we collect

We collect information that you choose to share with us, and a limited amount of technical information when you use the site.

  • Contact form: your name, email address, company, the service you are interested in, an indicative budget range and your message.
  • Email and phone: the details you provide when you contact us directly, such as your name, contact details and the content of your message.
  • Job enquiries: any information you send us about your experience when you enquire about a role, such as a CV or covering note.
  • Technical information: details such as your IP address, browser type, device and the pages you visit, which are processed when your browser loads the site.
03

How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • respond to your enquiry and discuss how we can help
  • prepare proposals and, where you become a client, deliver and manage our services
  • consider job enquiries and manage recruitment
  • keep the website working properly and understand how it can be improved
  • meet our legal, accounting and regulatory obligations

We rely on the following lawful bases under UK data protection law: our legitimate interests in running and developing our business, taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, performing a contract with you, complying with legal obligations, and your consent where we specifically ask for it.

04

Website and contact enquiries

When you submit our contact form, the details you enter are sent to us by email so that we can reply. The form is delivered through a third-party form processing service, which handles your submission only to pass it on to us.

Please only include the information we need to understand your enquiry. There is no need to send sensitive personal information through the form.

05

Communication

We will use your contact details to reply to you and to continue any conversation you have started with us.

We will not send you marketing emails unless you have agreed to receive them or we are otherwise permitted to do so by law. You can ask us to stop at any time by replying to one of our emails or contacting hello@nexyraconsulting.co.uk.

06

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it where this is needed to run our business, for example with:

  • providers that help us operate the website, handle form submissions and manage email
  • professional advisers, such as accountants or legal advisers, where needed
  • public authorities, where we are required to do so by law

Anyone we share information with is expected to keep it secure and to use it only for the purpose we shared it for.

07

Third-party services

Some parts of this website are delivered by third-party providers, which means your browser connects directly to their servers and shares technical information such as your IP address:

  • Our typeface is loaded from Google Fonts.
  • The map on our Locations page loads code and map data from a public content delivery network.
  • Links to our social media pages take you to those platforms, which apply their own privacy policies.

These providers handle information according to their own privacy policies.

08

International transfers

Some of the providers we use may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where this happens, we aim to use providers that offer appropriate safeguards for personal information, as required by UK data protection law.

09

Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access, in proportion to the information we hold. No method of transmitting or storing information online is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10

Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected. Enquiries that do not lead to a working relationship are kept for a reasonable period in case you get back in touch, and then deleted. Where you become a client, we keep records for as long as we need them to deliver our services and to meet legal and accounting requirements.

11

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over your personal information, including the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete
  • ask us to delete your information
  • ask us to restrict or stop using your information, or object to how we use it
  • ask us to transfer your information to another organisation, where this applies
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent

To make a request, please email hello@nexyraconsulting.co.uk. We will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can also contact the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

01

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website can store on your device when you visit it. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in a comparable way. They can be used to make a website work, remember your preferences or understand how a site is used.

02

How this website uses cookies

We keep our use of cookies to a minimum. This website does not currently use cookies for analytics, advertising or tracking you across other websites.

03

Essential functionality

The website may use technologies that are strictly necessary for it to work properly, for example to keep a page displaying correctly during your visit. These do not require your consent, and blocking them may affect how the site works.

04

Analytics and performance

We do not currently use analytics or performance cookies. If we introduce them in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before setting any cookies that require it.

05

Third-party services

Some content on this website is delivered by third-party providers. When your browser loads this content, it connects to their servers, and those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address:

  • Our typeface is loaded from Google Fonts.
  • The map on our Locations page loads code and map data from a public content delivery network.
  • If you follow links to our social media pages, those platforms may set their own cookies under their own policies.

We do not control how these providers use cookies or similar technologies. Please refer to their own policies for more information.

06

Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block all cookies, block third-party cookies only, or clear cookies when you close the browser. Your browser's help pages explain how to do this.

Blocking some types of cookies may affect how websites work, including this one.

07

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the way the website uses cookies changes. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

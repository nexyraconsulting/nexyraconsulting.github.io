About Adda Slough

Adda Slough originated from the very idea of gathering for an extended, relaxed and unlimited exchange of views in August 2012 by a close group of families. Later with the time as our reach extended, we started organizing cultural and sports events throughout the year, not only friends but for the community as well. The aim was to promote community integration and cohesion.

Adda Aid

Adda as a community and charitable trust has always looked to give back to the community in some way or form.

We have been able to create a community who could be reached out for any form of support. Adda as an organisation feels responsible to be able to help out communities effected due to any unforseen events.

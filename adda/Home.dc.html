About ADDA Slough

To promote cultural integration and sports within the community.

Adda Slough began in August 2012 with a close group of families gathering for a long, relaxed and unlimited exchange of views. As our reach grew, we started organising cultural and sports events throughout the year, for friends and for the whole community.

Adda Aid · Charity

Giving back, in Slough and in Bengal.

During Covid-19, Adda Slough worked with Slough Mitra Mandal, Slough Food Bank, Slough Hindu Temple and Sewa Day to deliver food, essentials and medicines to the doorsteps of the elderly, the vulnerable, health workers and stranded Indian students. With the help of Slough Council and Thames Valley Police, we reached a total of 50,000 people alongside our partners.

After Super Cyclone Amphan, ADDA raised funds globally and teamed up with Kolkata Mary Ward Social Centre to bring relief to the Sundarbans.

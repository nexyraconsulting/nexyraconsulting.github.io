Common questions

How were these prices set?

Each package is costed from the seniority and number of days the work genuinely requires, benchmarked against UK 2026 rates for senior research, design, brand and transformation specialists. We do not apply a flat hourly rate to every discipline.

What does a revision round mean?

A round is one consolidated set of feedback from your side, returned against a defined deliverable, and one revision from ours. Rounds are stated in every package so there is no ambiguity about what is included.

Do prices include VAT?

All prices are exclusive of UK VAT at the prevailing rate. VAT invoices are issued to UK-registered clients; overseas clients are invoiced net of UK VAT.

Why is Managed Services not fixed-price?

Managed Services is continuous capability rather than a defined deliverable, so it is priced monthly through the Partnership Ladder. Everything else on the Services page has a fixed-scope package.

What happens if our requirements change?

Anything outside the agreed scope is handled as a change request with its own fixed fee, agreed in writing before work proceeds. Nothing reaches your invoice without prior sign-off.

Can we combine services?

Yes. Most engagements combine two or three: research into brand, or transformation into managed delivery. Combined scopes are priced as a single programme rather than as separate packages added together.

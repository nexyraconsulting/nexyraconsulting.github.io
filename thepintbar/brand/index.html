01 — Primary lockup

Default signature for websites, headers and email footers. “OYO Entertainment” is set in Jost geometric sans directly under the wordmark and letter-spaced to span its exact width, so the two lock together as one block; the tagline and its brass rules sit below, unchanged. Minimum width 180px / 48mm.

08 — Endorsement line

OYO Entertainment is set in Jost Medium, all caps, tracking +0.44em — a geometric sans chosen to sit quietly against the Bodoni wordmark rather than compete with it. Never larger than the tagline; never above the wordmark. On its own it may be used as a standalone parent-company signature.
